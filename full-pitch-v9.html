Why Wellness Is the Business to Own Right Now

If you're going to own a business, own one in a market that keeps growing, with a model that doesn't need you inside it. Wellness in Singapore is that market.

  • A customer base that keeps growing

    More than 1 in 5 Singapore citizens are 65 or older today, and by 2035 it will be more than 1 in 4. Many people start paying closer attention to their wellbeing in their 40s too, so the people walking into your outlet are a lot like you. You'll understand exactly why they come.

  • A routine, not a fad

    A food concept can be the talk of the town one year and gone the next. Wellness is something people build into their week, so the members your outlet wins this year are still booking next year.

  • Lean by design

    Most outlets struggle because of what they cost before a single customer walks in: salaries, a big shopfront and their own advertising. Harmony Wellness has none of those, so less has to go right before your outlet starts earning.
