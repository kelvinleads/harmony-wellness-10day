The Maths Is Simple. That's the Point.

An outlet earns when members use the rooms. So what it makes comes down to three things you can see and question: how many rooms, how often they're booked, and what members pay.

Then come the running costs: rent, utilities, cleaning and marketing. No staff salaries, which is usually the biggest cost of all.

No secret formula. Just numbers you can test against your own common sense.
